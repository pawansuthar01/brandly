## Reminder tones

- `akx_*.wav`: Notification tones from [akx/Notifications](https://github.com/akx/Notifications), released under the repository's CC0 Public Domain option. The downloaded WAVs are bundled as preview tones; their `_alarm.wav` counterparts are normalized/looped locally for the app's two-minute reminder alarm.
